# Office examples

Examples are workshops for concrete projects. They may contain domain language,
project assumptions, fixtures, planners, and composed commands.

Use only public Office Cursor commands when the pack already exposes the needed
mechanism. If direct COM or OOXML work is unavoidable, first decide whether the
missing operation belongs in the pack; keep project-specific policy here.

Never add private client documents or identifying source material to a public
example. Generated outputs should be reproducible and clearly separated from
source fixtures.
